\begin{abstract}
This manuscript is a pre-results research scaffold. No empirical finding is
claimed. The planned study tests whether a validated subdiffusive
time-fractional Black--Scholes model improves out-of-sample option repricing in
liquidity and waiting-time regimes where the model's mechanism predicts slower
market time.
\end{abstract}
